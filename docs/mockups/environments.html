What I need from you

  1. One environment per window, or several? If one, step 2 is likely the whole feature.
  2. Stack, grid, or a mode on one object? Mode is cheaper and covers both, at the price of one key and one persisted field.
  3. Does a group own argv? If restart-one is the point, the group becomes a live layout and step 2 is not enough.
  4. Name. "stack", "group", "env", "set". It lands in ids, config keys, sidebar rows and docs.
  5. Shelf or group, not both? If the wildcard wins, step 3 may be dead code: the shelf is the same object with a rect that only exists while summoned.
