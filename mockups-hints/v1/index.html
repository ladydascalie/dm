dm hint bar · v1

One component for every shortcut row. All widths are terminal cells at the rail palette: keys in bar accent, labels in rail text, separators in rail dim, band on the rail hover ground.

The consolidation. dm paints shortcuts three ways today: the dashboard's separated row, the ask float's filled key caps, and the dim plain text inset in a box border. Those become one bar with two styles and two placements. The border placement is the row saver: the shortcuts ride the box's bottom rule instead of owning a row.

Narrow rule

The bar drops hints whole, never a key or half a label. Order is the caller's, except a hint marked keep. Multi-key tokens stay whole: ctrl+u/ctrl+d is one token. Pipes separate the hints that remain; a dropped hint takes its pipe with it.

Right-side status

A bar may pin one short status block at the right edge. The left hints fit around it and drop before it is touched, because the status is the only place that value appears.
